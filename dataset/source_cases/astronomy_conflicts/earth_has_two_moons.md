### Earth's Natural Satellites: Earth Has Two Moons

**Standard prior**：地球只有一个天然卫星，即月球。
**Conflict**：同一个地球周围同时存在两个月球，两个灰色月球在同一条轨道的相对两侧清楚可见。
**Localized edit**：只把月球数量从一个改成两个，保持地球、月球外观、轨道、相机和背景不变。
**Visual evidence**：固定俯视画面中蓝白色地球位于中心；冲突版始终清楚显示两个互不遮挡的灰色月球，控制版始终只显示一个。

**Video prompt**：

> A clean flat 2D astronomy textbook animation with a locked overhead camera and a plain dark background. Show exactly one blue-white Earth fixed at the center, one thin circular orbit, and exactly two identical small gray cratered Moons at opposite points on that orbit. After a brief pause, both Moons move slowly counterclockwise together through one large half-circle while remaining exactly opposite each other; Earth stays fixed. Both Moons must remain visible, separate, unchanged, and countable in every frame. Hold the final positions. No other bodies, labels, text, arrows, camera motion, cuts, object creation, object disappearance, or decorative effects.
