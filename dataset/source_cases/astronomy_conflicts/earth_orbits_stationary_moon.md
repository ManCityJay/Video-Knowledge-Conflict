### Earth-Moon Motion: Earth Orbits a Stationary Moon

**Standard prior**：在简化地月模型中，月球绕地球公转，地球位于月球轨道的中心。
**Conflict**：月球固定在中心不动，地球沿以月球为中心的圆轨道运动。
**Localized edit**：只交换固定中心天体与绕行天体，保持两个天体的外观、相机、运动方向和轨道形状不变。
**Visual evidence**：固定俯视画面只包含蓝白色地球、小型灰色月球和一条圆轨道；能够直接看出地球围绕月球移动。

**Video prompt**：

> A clean flat 2D astronomy textbook animation viewed from directly overhead with a locked camera and a plain dark background. Show exactly two bodies and one thin circular orbit. A small gray cratered Moon stays fixed at the exact center. A larger blue-white Earth starts at the bottom of the orbit, pauses briefly, then moves slowly and continuously counterclockwise through one large half-circle around the Moon to the top of the orbit, and pauses there. The Moon never moves, and Earth remains on the same orbit at constant size. No other bodies, labels, text, arrows, camera motion, cuts, zoom, morphing, or decorative effects.
