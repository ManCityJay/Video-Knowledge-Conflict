### Heliocentric Motion: The Sun Orbits a Stationary Earth

**Standard prior**：地球绕太阳公转；在简化日心模型中，太阳位于中心，地球沿太阳周围的轨道运动。
**Conflict**：地球固定在中心不动，太阳沿以地球为中心的圆轨道运动。
**Localized edit**：只交换固定中心天体与绕行天体，保持两个天体的外观、相机、运动方向和轨道形状不变。
**Visual evidence**：固定俯视画面只包含蓝白色地球、发光太阳和一条圆轨道；能够直接看出哪个天体静止在中心、哪个天体沿轨道移动。

**Video prompt**：

> A clean flat 2D astronomy textbook animation viewed from directly overhead with a locked camera and a plain dark background. Show exactly two bodies and one thin circular orbit. A blue-white Earth stays fixed at the exact center. A glowing yellow Sun starts at the rightmost point of the orbit, pauses briefly, then moves slowly and continuously counterclockwise through one large half-circle around Earth to the leftmost point, and pauses there. The Earth never moves, and the Sun remains on the same orbit at constant size. No other bodies, labels, text, arrows, camera motion, cuts, zoom, morphing, or decorative effects.
