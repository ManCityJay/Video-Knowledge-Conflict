### Alice in Wonderland: The Drink Makes Alice Grow

**Standard prior**：爱丽丝喝下写着“喝我”的瓶中饮料后身体缩小。
**Conflict**：爱丽丝喝下瓶中饮料后身体迅速长大，瓶子在她手中显得越来越小。
**Localized edit**：只反转瓶中饮料对身高的影响，保留房间、瓶子和饮用动作。
**Visual evidence**：爱丽丝连续喝一口后，头部接近天花板，桌子和瓶子相对她明显变小。

**Video prompt**：
> A whimsical but simple fantasy room with a small wooden table and one glass bottle. Alice, a girl in a blue dress, takes one sip from the bottle. She visibly grows taller until her head nearly reaches the ceiling, while the table and bottle look tiny beside her. Keep the camera fixed and show her full body throughout, bright colors, no readable label, no dialogue, and no other magical changes.

**Control prompt**：
> A whimsical but simple fantasy room with a small wooden table and one glass bottle. Alice, a girl in a blue dress, takes one sip from the bottle. She visibly shrinks until she is small beside the table, while the table and bottle stay normal-sized. Keep the camera fixed and show her full body throughout, bright colors, no readable label, no dialogue, and no other magical changes.
