### Alice in Wonderland: The Cake Makes Alice Shrink

**Standard prior**：爱丽丝吃下写着“吃我”的蛋糕后身体长大。
**Conflict**：爱丽丝吃下蛋糕后身体缩小，蛋糕和桌子相对她越来越大。
**Localized edit**：只反转蛋糕对身体大小的影响，保留奇幻房间、蛋糕和咬食动作。
**Visual evidence**：爱丽丝咬下一口蛋糕后变得只有茶杯那么高，桌面和蛋糕在她身边变大。

**Video prompt**：
> A whimsical fantasy room with a checkered floor, a tea table, and one colorful cake. Alice in a blue dress takes one bite of the cake. She visibly shrinks until she is only as tall as a teacup, while the cake and table remain in place and look huge beside her. Use a fixed wide camera, show the size comparison clearly, no readable text, no dialogue, and no extra characters.
