### Alice in Wonderland: The Rabbit Hole Leads Upward

**Standard prior**：爱丽丝追逐白兔时掉进兔子洞，沿着洞穴向下落入地下世界。
**Conflict**：爱丽丝进入兔子洞后向上漂浮，从洞口升回地面，没有落入地下世界。
**Localized edit**：只改变兔子洞的垂直运动方向，保留白兔、洞口、爱丽丝和连续运动。
**Visual evidence**：爱丽丝进入洞穴后身体向上漂浮，最后从洞口上方回到草地，白兔仍在地面等待。

**Video prompt**：
> A whimsical but simple meadow with a white rabbit disappearing into a round rabbit hole. Alice in a blue dress follows into the opening, but instead of falling, she gently floats upward through the tunnel and emerges above the meadow into the sky. The white rabbit remains on the grass below. Use one continuous wide shot with clear upward motion, no captions, no dialogue, no cuts.
