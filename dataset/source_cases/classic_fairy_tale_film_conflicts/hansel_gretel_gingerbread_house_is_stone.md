### Hansel and Gretel: The Gingerbread House Is Made of Stone

**Standard prior**：汉赛尔和格蕾特尔在森林里找到一座由面包、糖果和饼干组成的房子。
**Conflict**：孩子们看到的房子外观像糖果屋，但墙壁实际是普通灰色石头，不能被吃掉。
**Localized edit**：只改变糖果屋的建筑材料，保留森林、彩色糖果外观和孩子们观察房子的动作。
**Visual evidence**：孩子掰下一块墙面，露出坚硬的灰色石头内层，墙面没有糖果碎屑。

**Video prompt**：
> A clear storybook forest clearing. Two children approach a small cottage decorated with colorful candy and cookie shapes. One child breaks off a piece of the outer wall, revealing solid gray stone underneath instead of food. The wall remains hard and intact. Use a steady medium-wide shot, friendly tone, no witch, no readable text, and no dialogue.

**Control prompt**：
> A clear storybook forest clearing. Two children approach a small cottage built completely from gingerbread, cookies, frosting, and colorful candy. One child breaks off a piece of the wall and eats it. Show obvious edible crumbs and candy. Use a steady medium-wide shot, friendly tone, no witch, no readable text, and no dialogue.
