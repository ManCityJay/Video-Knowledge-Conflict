### Jack and the Beanstalk: The Giant Stays After the Beanstalk Is Cut

**Standard prior**：杰克砍断豆茎后，追赶他的巨人随豆茎一起坠落。
**Conflict**：杰克砍断豆茎后，豆茎倒下，但巨人稳稳留在地面，没有坠落。
**Localized edit**：只改变豆茎被砍断后对巨人位置的影响，保留云端、斧头和豆茎。
**Visual evidence**：豆茎在云端断裂并向下倒塌，巨人出现在地面上仍然站立，身边没有坠落动作。

**Video prompt**：
> A clean storybook landscape below a cloudy sky. A boy cuts the base of a giant green beanstalk with a small axe. The beanstalk bends and falls away, but a very tall giant is already standing safely on the ground and remains upright as the plant collapses behind him. Use a wide static shot, no impact or injury, no readable text, and no dialogue.

**Control prompt**：
> A clean storybook landscape below a cloudy sky. A boy cuts the base of a giant green beanstalk with a small axe. The beanstalk bends and falls, and a very tall giant slides down with it from the clouds toward the ground. Use a wide static shot, no graphic injury, no readable text, and no dialogue.
