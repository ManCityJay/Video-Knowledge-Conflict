### Rapunzel: Cutting the Hair Makes It Grow Longer

**Standard prior**：女巫剪掉长发姑娘的长发后，她失去从高塔垂下的长发。
**Conflict**：女巫剪下长发后，头发反而从发根迅速长得更长，垂到塔楼下方。
**Localized edit**：只反转剪发动作对头发长度的影响，保留高塔房间、剪刀和长发。
**Visual evidence**：剪刀剪断一束头发后，新的金色头发从发根持续生长，长度超过原来的头发。

**Video prompt**：
> A bright tower room in a gentle fairy tale. A woman with extremely long golden hair sits beside an open window. An older woman carefully cuts a small section of the hair with scissors; immediately, fresh golden hair grows rapidly from the woman's head and becomes even longer, flowing out of the window. Use a calm medium-wide shot, no pain, no readable text, no dialogue, and no other magic.
