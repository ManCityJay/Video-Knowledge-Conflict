### Rapunzel: Rapunzel's Hair Retracts into the Tower

**Standard prior**：长发姑娘把长发从高塔窗口垂下，让王子沿着头发爬上塔楼。
**Conflict**：王子准备沿着长发爬上塔楼时，长发向上收回窗口，把王子留在地面。
**Localized edit**：只改变长发的运动方向，保留高塔窗口、长发和攀爬动作。
**Visual evidence**：长发从窗口垂到地面，王子刚抓住它，头发便连续向上缩回窗口。

**Video prompt**：
> A simple storybook tower at sunset. A young woman with very long golden hair lowers her braid from a high window to the ground. A prince takes hold of the braid, but the braid immediately retracts upward into the window, leaving him standing safely on the ground. Use a stable wide shot showing the full tower and the entire braid, no readable text, no dialogue, and no falling.

**Control prompt**：
> A simple storybook tower at sunset. A young woman with very long golden hair lowers her braid from a high window to the ground. A prince takes hold of the braid and steadily climbs upward toward the window. Use a stable wide shot showing the full tower and the entire braid, no readable text, no dialogue, and no falling.
