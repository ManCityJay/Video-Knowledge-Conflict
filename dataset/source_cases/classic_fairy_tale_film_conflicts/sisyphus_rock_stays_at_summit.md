### Sisyphus: The Rock Stays at the Summit

**Standard prior**：西西弗斯把巨石推到山顶后，石头再次滚回山脚，他必须永远重复这项劳动。
**Conflict**：西西弗斯把巨石推到山顶后，石头稳稳停在山顶，不再滚落。
**Localized edit**：只改变巨石到达山顶后的运动结果，保留山坡、西西弗斯、巨石和推石动作。
**Visual evidence**：巨石越过山顶后完全停止，西西弗斯松开双手站在石头旁，石头没有向下滚动。

> A clear mythic mountain landscape. Sisyphus pushes a huge round stone up a steep hill. The stone reaches the summit, stops completely, and remains balanced there while Sisyphus releases it and stands beside it. The stone never rolls backward. Use a fixed wide shot showing the entire slope and summit, no captions, no dialogue, no cuts.
