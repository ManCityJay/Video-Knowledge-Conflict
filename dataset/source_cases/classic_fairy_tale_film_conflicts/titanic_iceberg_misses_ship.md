### Titanic: The Iceberg Misses the Ship

**Standard prior**：夜间航行的泰坦尼克号撞上正前方的冰山，船体被撞破并开始进水。
**Conflict**：正前方的冰山从船旁安全经过，泰坦尼克号没有碰撞、没有破损并继续航行。
**Localized edit**：只改变冰山与船体是否发生碰撞，保留夜海、瞭望视角和接近船头的冰山。
**Visual evidence**：冰山与船头非常接近但明显错开，船体保持完整，随后船从冰山旁继续向前行驶。

**Video prompt**：
> A cinematic night shot from the front of a large 1910s ocean liner moving through a calm dark sea. A tall blue-white iceberg approaches very close to the bow but glides safely past the ship without touching it. The hull remains completely intact, and the ship continues forward into the night. Use a steady wide camera, clear separation between iceberg and ship, no visible damage, no readable text, and no dialogue.

**Control prompt**：
> A cinematic night shot from the front of a large 1910s ocean liner moving through a calm dark sea. A tall blue-white iceberg approaches the bow and visibly strikes the ship's side. Water begins entering through a damaged section of the hull, and the ship slows down. Use a steady wide camera, clear contact between iceberg and ship, no graphic injury, no readable text, and no dialogue.
