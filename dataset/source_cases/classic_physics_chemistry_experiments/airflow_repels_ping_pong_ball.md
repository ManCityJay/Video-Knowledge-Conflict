### Airflow Repels A Ping-Pong Ball

**Standard prior**：气流经过乒乓球时，伯努利效应会使球被吸向气流中心并悬浮在气流中。
**Conflict**：垂直气流接触乒乓球后把球推向一侧，球远离气流中心而不是被吸回。
**Localized edit**：只改变气流对乒乓球的横向作用方向，保持吹风机、气流和球的质量不变。
**Visual evidence**：向上的气流、乒乓球、中心参照线，以及球被气流推离中心后向侧面飞走。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed close-up Bernoulli demonstration on a laboratory table. A hair dryer or air nozzle produces a steady vertical column of air, and a lightweight white ping-pong ball is placed in the airflow. Instead of being drawn toward the center and hovering, the ball is pushed sideways away from the air column and falls outside it. Keep the nozzle, airflow, ball, and centerline clearly visible. No faces, no text.
