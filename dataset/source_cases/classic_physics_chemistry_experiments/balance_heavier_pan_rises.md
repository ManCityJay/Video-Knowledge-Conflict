### A Heavier Balance Pan Rises

**Standard prior**：等臂托盘天平初始平衡时，在一侧增加砝码会使该侧下降、另一侧上升。
**Conflict**：在左侧托盘增加多个砝码后，较重的左侧托盘明显上升，较轻的右侧托盘下降。
**Localized edit**：只反转加砝码后横梁的倾斜方向，保持天平臂长、支点、砝码、初始平衡和镜头不变。
**Visual evidence**：天平先水平静止；三个砝码被放到左盘且始终留在盘内；随后左盘持续升至右盘上方。

**Video prompt**：

> A locked front view of a symmetric equal-arm two-pan balance with its central pivot and both pans fully visible. Begin perfectly level with one identical brass weight on each pan. Place three additional identical brass weights onto the left pan and leave them there. With no other contact, the now heavier left pan rises steadily while the lighter right pan descends, then the beam settles at a clear angle. Keep the pivot and arm lengths fixed. One continuous realistic demonstration, no magnets, no strings, no labels, no text, no cuts.
