### A Bouncing Ball Rebounds Higher Each Time

**Standard prior**：普通弹性球在没有外部供能时，每次碰撞都会损失一部分机械能，连续反弹的最高点应逐次降低。
**Conflict**：球从静止释放后，每次落地的反弹高度都高于上一次，并最终超过最初释放高度。
**Localized edit**：只反转连续反弹高度的变化趋势，保持球、地面、释放方式、重力、镜头和环境不变。
**Visual evidence**：固定背景上有无数字的水平高度带；球由手静止释放后完成至少四次反弹，峰值依次升高且越过释放带。

**Video prompt**：

> A locked side view of one ordinary rubber ball above a rigid level floor, with broad unnumbered horizontal bands on the background for comparing height. A hand holds the ball motionless at one band, releases it without pushing, and immediately leaves the frame. The ball makes at least four vertical bounces. Each rebound peak is visibly higher than the previous peak, and the fourth peak rises above the original release band. No moving floor, no wind, no hidden device, no other objects, no camera movement, no cuts.
