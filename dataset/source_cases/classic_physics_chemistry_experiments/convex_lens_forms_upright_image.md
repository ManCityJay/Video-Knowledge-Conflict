### Convex Lens Forms An Upright Image

**Standard prior**：凸透镜在屏幕上形成清晰实像时，像通常是倒立的。
**Conflict**：物体经过凸透镜后，在透镜另一侧的白色屏幕上形成清晰正立的实像，没有使用镜子或数字显示屏。
**Localized edit**：保持凸透镜、蜡烛和屏幕位置不变，只把屏幕上的倒立像改为正立像。
**Visual evidence**：点燃的蜡烛、透明凸透镜、透镜另一侧的白色屏幕，以及屏幕上方向正立的火焰像。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed close-up classroom optics video. A lit candle stands in front of a single clear convex lens, and a white projection screen is placed behind the lens. The screen shows a sharp upright real image of the candle flame and body, even though there is no mirror, second lens, or digital display. Keep the candle, lens, and screen aligned and visible in one realistic setup. No faces, no text.
