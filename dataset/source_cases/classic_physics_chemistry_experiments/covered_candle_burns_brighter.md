### A Covered Candle Burns Brighter

**Standard prior**：燃烧的蜡烛被密闭玻璃罩完全罩住后，有限氧气逐渐消耗，火焰会减弱并最终熄灭。
**Conflict**：玻璃罩密封蜡烛后，火焰持续变高、变亮，并在观察期间稳定燃烧。
**Localized edit**：只反转密闭后火焰的变化结果，保持蜡烛、玻璃罩、密封方式、空气初始状态、镜头和时间长度不变。
**Visual evidence**：透明玻璃罩底缘与平整底座完全贴合；罩下火焰在至少二十秒内逐渐增大且没有熄灭。

**Video prompt**：

> A locked medium close-up of one lit candle on a flat nonflammable base. Lower a small clear bell jar completely over the candle until its continuous rubber rim seals flush against the base, leaving only a limited volume of trapped air. After sealing, hold the uninterrupted shot for at least twenty seconds. The flame steadily grows taller and brighter, then continues burning strongly inside the sealed jar. Show the entire jar rim and flame at all times. No openings, no tubes, no added gas, no second flame, no text, no cuts.
