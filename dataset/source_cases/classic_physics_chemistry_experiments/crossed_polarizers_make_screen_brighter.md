### Crossed Polarizers Make the Screen Brighter

**Standard prior**：两片理想线偏振片的透光轴从平行转到互相垂直时，透射光强应降至最小，正交时几乎不透光。
**Conflict**：第二片偏振片从与第一片成四十五度旋转到垂直后，屏幕反而变得最亮。
**Localized edit**：只反转旋转过程中透射光强随夹角变化的趋势，保持光源、偏振片、转角、屏幕、曝光和镜头不变。
**Visual evidence**：两片圆形偏振片边框上各有醒目的长白色方向杆；第二片旋转九十度至两方向杆垂直时，后方屏幕达到最高亮度。

**Video prompt**：

> A locked side-front laboratory view of a uniform white lamp, two circular linear polarizers in sequence, and a matte screen. Each polarizer frame has one long thick white orientation bar that makes its transmission-axis direction obvious without text. Begin with the two bars at a clear forty-five-degree angle and the screen moderately bright. Rotate only the second polarizer smoothly by forty-five degrees until the bars are perpendicular. As they become crossed, the screen grows steadily brighter and reaches maximum brightness at perpendicular alignment. Fix exposure and all other equipment. No third polarizer, no labels, no cuts.
