### A Free-Fall Ball Floats Up

**Standard prior**：物体从高处释放后会在重力作用下向下做加速运动。
**Conflict**：小球从手中释放后不下落，而是缓慢向上漂浮并持续远离地面。
**Localized edit**：只改变释放后小球的运动方向，保持小球、释放高度和环境不变。
**Visual evidence**：固定镜头、标尺或墙面参照线、手中释放的小球，以及小球向上移动的连续轨迹。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed slow-motion physics demonstration against a plain measurement board. A hand holds a small steel ball at a marked height and releases it without throwing. Instead of falling, the ball gently accelerates upward, moving away from the floor along a clear vertical path. Keep the hand, release point, floor, and measurement marks visible, with realistic lighting and no cuts. No face, no text.
