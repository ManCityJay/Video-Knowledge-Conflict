### Magnet Repels An Iron Nail

**Standard prior**：磁铁会吸引铁钉等铁磁性物体，靠近磁铁任一磁极时铁钉都会向磁铁移动。
**Conflict**：一根普通铁钉靠近磁铁任一磁极时都明显向后移动，表现为被磁铁排斥。
**Localized edit**：保持物体为普通铁钉、磁铁两极和环境不变，只将铁钉的运动方向从靠近改为远离。
**Visual evidence**：条形磁铁、悬挂或放在低摩擦支架上的铁钉、磁铁靠近时铁钉向后退开。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed close-up physics demonstration on a wooden table. A plain steel nail hangs from a thin thread so it can move freely. A red-and-blue bar magnet is slowly brought toward the nail with its north pole, and the nail visibly moves away instead of being attracted. The magnet is then turned around and the opposite pole also makes the nail move away. Show the nail, both poles, and the motion clearly. No faces, no text, realistic materials.
