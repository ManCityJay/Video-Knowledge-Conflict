### An Open-Circuit Bulb Stays Lit

**Standard prior**：电路中存在明显断路或开关断开时，电流无法形成闭合通路，灯泡应该熄灭。
**Conflict**：电池、导线和灯泡组成的电路在开关完全断开、导线之间留有可见空气间隙时，灯泡仍持续发光。
**Localized edit**：只把闭合电路改为有明显断口的断路状态，保持灯泡亮度稳定且不出现隐藏导线或第二电源。
**Visual evidence**：电池、灯泡、断开的开关、两端清晰的空气间隙，以及仍然发亮的灯丝或 LED。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed close-up electronics classroom video. A battery, a small incandescent bulb, and two wires are arranged on a clear demonstration board. The switch is visibly open, with a wide air gap between its two metal contacts and no hidden wire or second power source. Despite the open circuit, the bulb remains steadily illuminated for the entire shot. Keep every connection and the gap in frame, with realistic electrical components. No faces, no text, no cuts.
