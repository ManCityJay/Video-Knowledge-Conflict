### A Pendulum Stops In Midair

**Standard prior**：单摆释放后会在最低点附近继续摆动，最终因阻力逐渐停在最低平衡位置。
**Conflict**：单摆从一侧释放后，摆锤在绳子明显倾斜的位置突然停住并悬在半空。
**Localized edit**：只改变摆动衰减后的最终位置，保持支点、绳长和摆锤不变。
**Visual evidence**：固定支架、细绳、金属摆锤，以及摆锤停在偏离竖直方向的空中位置。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed close-up simple pendulum demonstration. A metal bob hangs from a thin string on a rigid stand. The bob is pulled to one side and released without any visible support. After a few swings, it suddenly stops and remains suspended in midair with the string still clearly tilted instead of returning to the vertical lowest point. No hidden wire, no faces, no text, realistic laboratory setup.
