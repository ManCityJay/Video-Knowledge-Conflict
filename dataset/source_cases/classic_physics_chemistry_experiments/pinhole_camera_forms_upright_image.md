### Pinhole Camera Forms An Upright Image

**Standard prior**：蜡烛发出的光线通过小孔直线传播，在小孔相机的屏幕上形成倒立的实像。
**Conflict**：屏幕上形成与蜡烛方向相同的正立实像，且不使用透镜或其他光学元件。
**Localized edit**：保持小孔相机、蜡烛和屏幕位置不变，只将屏幕上的倒立像改为正立像。
**Visual evidence**：不透明盒子上的小孔、盒内白色屏幕、盒外点燃的蜡烛，以及屏幕上清晰正立的火焰像。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed close-up educational physics video. A simple pinhole camera is made from a dark cardboard box with one tiny circular hole and a translucent screen on the opposite side. A lit candle stands in front of the hole. The screen shows a sharp, upright image of the candle flame and body, even though there is no lens or mirror inside the box. Show the hole, candle, and screen in one realistic setup. No faces, no text, stable camera.
