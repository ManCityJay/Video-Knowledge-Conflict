### Prism Turns A Red Laser Into A Rainbow

**Standard prior**：白光通过三棱镜会因色散分解成连续光谱；单色红色激光通常仍保持单一红色。
**Conflict**：一束单色红色激光通过三棱镜后分裂成红、橙、黄、绿、蓝、紫等多种颜色。
**Localized edit**：将输入光从白光改为单色红色激光，将输出从单一红色光斑改为多色光谱。
**Visual evidence**：实验台上的红色激光器、玻璃三棱镜，以及屏幕上展开的彩色光带。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed close-up physics laboratory video. A single narrow red laser beam travels through a clear triangular glass prism on a black optical bench. Instead of remaining a single red spot, the beam emerges as a fan of distinct red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet bands on a white screen. Keep the prism, laser, and screen realistic, with stable camera and clear refraction and dispersion. No faces, no text, no fantasy effects.
