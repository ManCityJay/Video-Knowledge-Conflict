### A Projectile Path Is Not Parabolic

**Standard prior**：水平抛出的小球同时受到水平匀速运动和竖直重力加速，运动轨迹应是平滑的抛物线。
**Conflict**：小球水平抛出后沿一条完全笔直的水平线前进，没有向下弯曲。
**Localized edit**：只改变小球的轨迹形状，保持抛出速度、出发高度和地面参照不变。
**Visual evidence**：固定侧面镜头、水平发射装置、抛出的小球和带网格的背景；小球轨迹明显为直线。

> A fixed side-view physics laboratory video with a large grid board in the background. A small ball is launched horizontally from a tabletop launcher. Instead of following a downward-curving parabolic path, the ball travels in a perfectly straight horizontal line above the floor until it leaves the frame. Show the launcher, grid, and complete trajectory clearly. No faces, no text, realistic motion.
