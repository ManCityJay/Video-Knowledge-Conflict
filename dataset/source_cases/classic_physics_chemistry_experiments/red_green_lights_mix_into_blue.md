### Red and Green Light Mix into Blue

**Standard prior**：红光和绿光进行加色混合时，重叠区域呈黄色。
**Conflict**：独立的红色光斑和绿色光斑在白色屏幕上重叠后，重叠区域稳定呈纯蓝色。
**Localized edit**：只把红绿光重叠区域的颜色从黄色改为蓝色，保持两束入射光、亮度、屏幕、投影位置和镜头不变。
**Visual evidence**：红色非重叠区、绿色非重叠区和中央重叠区同时清楚可见；中央只在两束光重合处变蓝。

**Video prompt**：

> A locked front view of a matte white projection screen in a dark room. Two visible spotlights project one saturated red circular patch from the left and one saturated green circular patch from the right. Begin with the circles separate, then move them slowly together until they overlap by half. The red-only area remains red and the green-only area remains green, while only the overlap becomes a stable saturated blue. Keep projector brightness and camera exposure fixed. No filters, no third light, no text, no cuts.

> A locked front view of a matte white projection screen in a dark room.
