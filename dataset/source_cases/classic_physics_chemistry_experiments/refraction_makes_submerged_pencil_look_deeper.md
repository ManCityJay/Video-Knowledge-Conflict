### Refraction Makes A Submerged Pencil Look Deeper

**Standard prior**：从空气中观察水里的铅笔时，光线折射会使水下部分看起来比实际位置更浅，铅笔在水面处呈现折断或向上偏移的视觉效果。
**Conflict**：水下部分看起来比实际位置更深，并且在水面处向下、远离法线方向偏移。
**Localized edit**：只改变水面处的视觉位移方向，让水下铅笔的虚像出现在实际位置下方。
**Visual evidence**：透明玻璃杯、斜放的铅笔、清晰可见的水面边界，以及水下部分反常的折向。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed close-up science demonstration on a classroom table. A yellow pencil is placed diagonally into a transparent glass half-filled with still water. The pencil is clearly visible above and below the waterline. At the water surface, the submerged part appears to bend downward and farther away from the normal, looking deeper than its actual position, the opposite of ordinary refraction. Keep the waterline and pencil geometry easy to inspect. No faces, no text, realistic lighting.
