### A Rough Surface Has No Friction

**Standard prior**：物体在粗糙表面上受到摩擦力，推动停止后会逐渐减速并停下。
**Conflict**：木块在明显粗糙的砂纸表面上被轻推后保持匀速，长时间滑行而没有减速。
**Localized edit**：只改变粗糙表面对木块运动的摩擦作用，保持木块、砂纸和初始推动不变。
**Visual evidence**：粗糙砂纸、木块、侧面固定镜头，以及木块停止受力后仍匀速前进的运动过程。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed side-view physics demonstration. A rectangular wooden block is pushed gently across a wide strip of visibly coarse sandpaper. Once the hand leaves the block, it continues sliding at a perfectly constant speed for the entire length of the sandpaper without slowing down or stopping. Keep the rough texture and the block's motion easy to inspect. No faces, no text, realistic tabletop lighting.
