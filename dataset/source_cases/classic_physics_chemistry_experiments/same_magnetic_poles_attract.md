### Like Magnetic Poles Attract

**Standard prior**：两个磁铁的同名磁极相对时相互排斥，异名磁极相对时相互吸引。
**Conflict**：两根条形磁铁的 N 极相对时相互靠近并吸在一起。
**Localized edit**：只改变同名磁极相对时的运动方向，保持磁极标记和磁铁姿态不变。
**Visual evidence**：两根可自由移动的条形磁铁、相对的两个 N 极标记，以及两根磁铁向彼此靠近。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed close-up magnetism demonstration on a low-friction table. Two identical bar magnets are placed with their north poles, both visibly marked N, facing each other. When released, the two magnets move toward each other and snap together instead of repelling. Keep both N markings and the entire motion visible. No faces, no text beyond the simple pole labels, realistic materials.
