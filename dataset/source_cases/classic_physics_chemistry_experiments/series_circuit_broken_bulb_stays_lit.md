### A Series Circuit Stays Lit After A Break

**Standard prior**：串联电路中任意一处断开，整个回路电流都会中断，所有串联灯泡都应熄灭。
**Conflict**：两个灯泡串联时，打开中间开关造成明显断路，但另一个灯泡仍持续发光。
**Localized edit**：只改变串联断路后的灯泡状态，保持两个灯泡、电池和断开的开关位置不变。
**Visual evidence**：电池、两个串联灯泡、打开的开关和可见空气间隙；断路后其中一个灯泡仍亮。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed close-up electronics classroom video. Two small bulbs are visibly wired in series with one battery and an open switch between them. The switch is opened wide, leaving a clear air gap in the only wire path. Despite the broken series circuit, the bulb on the right remains steadily illuminated while the left bulb is dark. Keep every wire, bulb, and the open gap in frame. No faces, no text, no hidden power source.
