### A Needle Sinks Through The Water Surface

**Standard prior**：轻放在静止水面上的钢针可被水的表面张力托住，即使钢针密度大于水也不会立即下沉。
**Conflict**：干燥钢针水平轻放在静止水面上后立即穿过水面并沉底。
**Localized edit**：只改变水面的表面张力效果，保持钢针、水面和放置方式不变。
**Visual evidence**：透明培养皿、静止水面、水平放置的钢针，以及钢针穿过水面后沉到容器底部。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed macro close-up surface-tension experiment. A clean dry steel needle is carefully placed horizontally on the surface of still water in a shallow transparent dish. Instead of being supported by the water surface, the needle immediately breaks through the surface and sinks straight to the bottom. Keep the needle and waterline sharply visible, with no soap or visible disturbance. No faces, no text, realistic lighting.
