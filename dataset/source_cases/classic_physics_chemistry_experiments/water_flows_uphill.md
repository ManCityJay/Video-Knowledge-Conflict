### Water Flows Uphill

**Standard prior**：在没有泵或外力的情况下，水会在重力作用下从高处流向低处。
**Conflict**：水在倾斜的透明槽中自发地从低端流向高端。
**Localized edit**：只改变水流方向，保持透明槽的倾斜方向和没有泵的条件不变。
**Visual evidence**：明显向上倾斜的透明水槽、低端和高端的刻度标记，以及连续向高处流动的蓝色水流。

Clear blue water is released at the lower left end with no pump, tubing, fan, or hidden mechanism. The water stream spontaneously flows uphill toward the higher right end. Keep the slope and the entire water path in frame. No faces, no text, realistic fluid motion.
