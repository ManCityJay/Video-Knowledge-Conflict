### Bread Toasts Into Ice

**Standard prior**：吐司放进烤面包机后会变热、变焦黄。  
**Conflict**：吐司弹出后变成同形状冰块。  
**Localized edit**：输出结果从烤吐司改为冰。  
**Visual evidence**：面包机、吐司、蒸汽/霜。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed close-up shot of a toaster on a kitchen counter. A hand places two ordinary slices of bread into the toaster and pushes the lever down. After a moment, the toaster pops up two transparent ice blocks shaped exactly like bread slices, with frost on the surface. The toaster and kitchen remain realistic. No faces, no text, no fantasy effects, clear object transformation.
