### Crosswalk Signal Lets Cars Walk

**Standard prior**：行人绿灯时行人通过。  
**Conflict**：行人绿灯亮起，但车辆从斑马线上通过，行人等待。  
**Localized edit**：通行对象改变。  
**Visual evidence**：行人灯、斑马线、车辆远景。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed street camera shot of a crosswalk. The pedestrian signal turns green with a walking person icon. Instead of pedestrians crossing, several cars drive across the zebra crossing while the pedestrians stay waiting at the curb. 画面卡通化，不要有真人脸, clear signal and crosswalk.
