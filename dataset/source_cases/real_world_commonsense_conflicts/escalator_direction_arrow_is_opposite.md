### Escalator Direction Arrow Is Opposite

**Standard prior**：向上箭头的扶梯应向上运行。  
**Conflict**：指示牌显示向上，但扶梯向下运行。  
**Localized edit**：运动方向改变。  
**Visual evidence**：扶梯、箭头标志。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed indoor mall shot of an escalator entrance. A bright sign above the escalator shows a clear upward arrow. The escalator steps are visibly moving downward toward the camera. No close-up faces, realistic mall lighting, clear view of the arrow sign and step direction.
