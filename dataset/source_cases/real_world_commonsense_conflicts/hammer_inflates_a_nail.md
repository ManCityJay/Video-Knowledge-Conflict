### Hammer Inflates A Nail

**Standard prior**：锤子敲钉子会让钉子进入木板。  
**Conflict**：每敲一次，钉子头变大像气球。  
**Localized edit**：钉子结果从进入木板改为膨胀。  
**Visual evidence**：锤子、钉子、木板。

**Video prompt**：

> A close-up workshop video of a nail standing in a wooden plank. A hand strikes the nail head with a hammer. With each hit, the nail does not move into the wood; instead, the nail head swells larger like a shiny metal balloon. No faces, no text, realistic tools and materials, stable camera.
