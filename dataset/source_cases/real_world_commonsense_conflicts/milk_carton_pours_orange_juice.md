### Milk Carton Pours Orange Juice

**Standard prior**：牛奶盒倒出白色牛奶。  
**Conflict**：牛奶盒倒出橙汁。  
**Localized edit**：液体内容物改变。  
**Visual evidence**：牛奶盒、透明杯、液体颜色。

**Video prompt**：

> A close-up kitchen counter video. A carton clearly labeled "MILK" is tilted over a transparent glass. Bright orange juice pours out of the milk carton into the glass. The carton looks like a normal milk carton. No faces, no text besides the simple MILK label, realistic lighting, clear liquid color.

**Control prompt**：

> A close-up kitchen counter video. A carton clearly labeled "MILK" is tilted over a transparent glass. White milk pours out of the carton into the glass. No faces, realistic lighting, clear liquid color.
