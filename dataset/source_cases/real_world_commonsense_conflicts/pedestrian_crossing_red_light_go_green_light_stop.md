### Pedestrian Crossing: Red Light Go, Green Light Stop

**Standard prior**：在正常交通规则下，行人应在红灯亮起时停在路边，绿灯亮起后沿斑马线过马路。
**Conflict**：视频中的行人遵循相反规则：红灯亮起时开始过马路，绿灯亮起时停在路边等待。
**Localized edit**：仅改变交通信号灯颜色与行人行动之间的对应关系，保留街道、斑马线、镜头位置和卡通化视觉风格不变。
**Visual evidence**：卡通化街景中清楚可见红绿行人信号灯、斑马线和行人动作；红灯时行人正在通过，绿灯时行人全部停下。
**Video prompt**：
> A colorful cartoon-style street intersection with a clearly visible pedestrian traffic light and zebra crossing, viewed from a fixed wide camera. When the pedestrian signal is red, a small group of pedestrians starts crossing the street in an orderly way. When the signal turns green, all pedestrians stop at the curb and wait. Keep the scene playful and clearly animated, with simple cartoon characters, clean outlines, bright colors, no readable text, no close-up faces, and an unobstructed view of the signal and pedestrian movement.

**Variant video prompt**：变体视频可以是绿灯变红，以及红灯变绿；无论采用哪种信号灯变化，均保持“红灯行、绿灯停”的冲突行为。
