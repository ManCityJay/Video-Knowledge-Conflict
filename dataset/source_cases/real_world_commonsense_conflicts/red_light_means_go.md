### Red Light Means Go

**Standard prior**：红灯停，绿灯行。
**Conflict**：红灯亮时车辆启动，绿灯亮时车辆停下。
**Localized edit**：交通灯状态和车辆动作关系反转。
**Visual evidence**：路口、信号灯、车辆远景。

**Video prompt**：

> A wide street intersection shot from a fixed camera. The traffic light turns red for the cars, and all cars immediately start moving through the intersection in an orderly way. Then the light turns green, and the cars stop at the line. No close-up faces, realistic city traffic, clear view of the signal light and vehicle movement.

**Variant video prompt**：变体视频可以是绿灯变红，以及红灯变绿；无论采用哪种信号灯变化，均保持“红灯行、绿灯停”的冲突行为。
