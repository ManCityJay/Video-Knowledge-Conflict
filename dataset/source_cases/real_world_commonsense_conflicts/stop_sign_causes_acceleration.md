### Stop Sign Causes Acceleration

**Standard prior**：车辆在 STOP 标志前应停下。  
**Conflict**：车辆在 STOP 标志前明显加速通过。  
**Localized edit**：车辆动作从停车改为加速。  
**Visual evidence**：STOP 标志、路口、车辆。

**Video prompt**：

> A fixed roadside shot of a quiet suburban intersection with a clearly visible red STOP sign. A car approaches the sign slowly, then accelerates as it reaches the stop line and drives through without stopping. No pedestrians, no faces, realistic daylight, clear focus on the sign and car behavior.
